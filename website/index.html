Who it's for

Built for how healthcare actually works in your market

From busy polyclinics to multi-department hospitals — KayCare adapts to your facility size, workflow, and local insurance requirements.

Private Clinics

Stop juggling paper, spreadsheets, and a separate billing book

A small clinic with 2–5 doctors needs the same core workflow as a hospital: register patients, write prescriptions, record lab results, and collect payment. KayCare gives you all of that — without the enterprise price tag.

  • Patient records and MRN from day one
  • Bills auto-generated from consultations
  • Clinic tier starts at GHS 600/month
District & Government Hospitals

Insurance claim tracking without a separate spreadsheet

Government facilities deal with high insurance patient volumes and the manual overhead of tracking claims. KayCare captures payer details at registration, tags every bill, and lets you monitor claim status in one place.

  • Insurance / scheme number captured at registration
  • Claim status tracked per bill (Draft → Submitted → Approved)
  • AR aging report flags overdue and unpaid claims
Polyclinics

Multiple departments, one system, no double entry

Polyclinics serve a broad mix of outpatients across OPD, lab, and pharmacy — often with shift handovers and role-specific access. KayCare's role-based access means each staff member sees only what they need.

  • 6 roles: Doctor, Nurse, Pharmacist, Lab, Receptionist, Admin
  • Lab order flows automatically from consultation
  • Full audit log — every action, every user, timestamped
Faith-Based & Mission Hospitals

Affordable, cloud-based — no on-site server required

Mission hospitals often run lean IT budgets and can't maintain local servers. KayCare is fully cloud-hosted — no hardware to buy, no backups to manage, and a price designed for facilities that prioritise community care over margins.

  • Zero hardware — runs on any browser, any device
  • Data hosted in Africa — low latency for Ghana
  • Discounts & waiver tracking for subsidised care
