Clinical modules

One system. Every department.

From reception to the pharmacy, every department works in the same system — patient data flows between them automatically.

Patient Management

Complete patient records with auto-generated MRN, allergy tracking, insurance details, scheme number, and full visit history.

  • · Auto-generated MRN (MRN-{YEAR}-{seq})
  • · Drug allergy register with severity tracking
  • · Insurance scheme number + policy fields
  • · Full appointment and consultation history

Appointments

Schedule, reschedule, and track patient appointments across all doctors with a full state machine enforcing valid transitions.

  • · Status: Booked → Confirmed → Attended / Cancelled
  • · Auto-completes on consultation sign-off
  • · Filter by doctor, date, and status
  • · Receptionist-friendly workflow

Consultations

Structured SOAP notes with primary and secondary diagnoses, vitals, and a sign-off workflow that triggers charge capture automatically.

  • · SOAP note structure (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan)
  • · Multiple secondary diagnoses
  • · Vitals recording (BP, temp, weight, SpO₂)
  • · Sign-off triggers auto charge capture

Prescriptions

Full prescribing workflow with autocomplete, allergy warnings, templates, partial dispense, and downloadable PDF prescriptions.

  • · Medication autocomplete with allergy cross-check
  • · Real-time drug allergy warnings
  • · 30-day expiry with auto-status update
  • · Partial dispense with history tracking
  • · Downloadable A4 PDF prescription
  • · Doctor prescription templates (personal + shared)

Billing & Revenue

Complete revenue cycle management — from auto charge capture through insurance claims, discounts, credit notes, and detailed financial reporting.

  • · Auto charge capture (consultation, lab, Rx)
  • · Insurance / payer claim tracking
  • · Discounts, waivers, and adjustments
  • · Credit notes and refunds
  • · PDF invoice + payment receipt
  • · Revenue dashboard + AR aging report
  • · Bill templates / packages

Laboratory (LIS)

Full Laboratory Information System with HL7 instrument integration, reference ranges, auto-flagging, and professional PDF lab reports.

  • · Lab order management and test catalog
  • · HL7 v2 instrument integration (MLLP port 2575)
  • · Reference ranges with H/L/N auto-flagging
  • · Manual result entry with validation
  • · Downloadable A4 PDF lab report
  • · Department-grouped results table

Pharmacy Inventory

Real-time drug inventory with stock movements, reorder alerts, and automatic deduction when prescriptions are dispensed.

  • · Drug catalog with dosage form, strength, category
  • · Reorder threshold alerts per drug
  • · Stock movements: receive, dispense, adjust, return, expire
  • · Auto-deduction on prescription dispense
  • · Controlled substance (CS) flags
  • · Reorder alerts dashboard grouped by category

Documents

Securely upload and retrieve patient documents with time-limited, read-only access links — accessible only to authorised staff.

  • · Upload referral letters, imaging, reports
  • · Per-facility isolated storage
  • · Expiring access links (15-min)
  • · Never publicly accessible

Audit & Compliance

Every patient record access, creation, and update is logged automatically. Full staff accountability built into the platform.

  • · Immutable audit log (BIGINT PK, no FK)
  • · Records: who, what, when, patient
  • · Patient.View, Patient.Create, Patient.Update events
  • · Survives tenant deletion
Platform

Built to run a real hospital

The infrastructure and platform capabilities that make KayCare production-ready from day one.

Multi-Facility, One Platform

Every facility gets its own fully isolated environment. Strict data separation is enforced at multiple layers — no data ever crosses facility boundaries, guaranteed.

Auto Charge Capture

Every clinical action automatically generates a billing charge — consultation sign-off, lab order placement, and prescription dispense each add a line to the patient's bill. Charges appear instantly, with "Auto" badge on the bill.

PDF Reports Throughout

Downloadable PDFs for lab results, prescriptions, invoices, and payment receipts — all carrying your facility's name, logo, address, and contact details. Configured once in Facility Settings, applied everywhere.

Role-Based Access Control

Six roles — Doctor, Nurse, Receptionist, Pharmacist, Admin, SuperAdmin — each with precisely scoped permissions enforced at every API endpoint. Staff only see and do what their role allows.

Financial Reporting

Revenue dashboard, AR aging report (Current / 30 / 60 / 90+ days), payment tracking by method (cash, mobile money, card, insurance, bank transfer), and service price catalog. Real-time visibility into where your money is.

Admin & User Management

Full staff management from the UI — create accounts, assign roles, manage departments, reset passwords, deactivate staff. Facility settings (logo, name, address) configure what appears on all PDFs and headers.

Built for emerging markets

Designed for how
your healthcare system works

Most HMS software is built for Western healthcare systems and retrofitted for everyone else. KayCare was designed from the ground up for hospitals in emerging markets — the payment methods, the insurance model, the workflows that actually reflect how care is delivered.

  • Local insurance claim tracking

    Track claims per bill, record insurer approvals, and split patient vs. insurance portions. Works with any national or private health scheme.

  • Flexible currency support

    Billing, pricing, and reporting in your local currency. No conversion confusion — your staff see the numbers they work with every day.

  • Prescription autocomplete with allergy checks

    Medication search with real-time allergy cross-checking — doctors prescribe faster and safely, with expiry tracking and partial dispense built in.

  • All payment methods supported

    Record payments by cash, mobile money, card, insurance, bank transfer, or cheque — however your patients pay, we track it.
