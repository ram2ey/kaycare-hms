Our story

We built what
emerging-market hospitals needed

Most hospital software is built for NHS trusts or American health systems and then "adapted" for everywhere else. We started from scratch — designing every screen, every workflow, and every default around how care actually gets delivered in emerging markets.

The problem we're solving

Hospitals in emerging markets were managing on spreadsheets and paper

Walk into most private clinics or district hospitals across Africa, South Asia, or Southeast Asia and you'll find patient registers in exercise books, billing done in Excel, and lab results handwritten on request forms. Not because staff aren't capable — because the software available was either priced for large institutions or built for healthcare systems that look nothing like theirs.

The consequences are real: charges get missed, insurance claims get lost, drug stockouts happen without warning, and audit trails don't exist. KayCare was built to fix all of that — at a price point that works for a district hospital, not just a teaching hospital.

Before KayCare
  • ✗ Patient records in paper registers
  • ✗ Billing in Excel — charges missed daily
  • ✗ NHIS claims tracked manually, often lost
  • ✗ Drug stockouts discovered at the dispense counter
  • ✗ No audit trail — no staff accountability
  • ✗ Lab results on paper, results delayed or lost
After KayCare
  • ✓ Searchable patient records, accessible from any device
  • ✓ Every clinical action auto-generates a charge
  • ✓ NHIS claims tracked per bill with approval status
  • ✓ Reorder alerts before stock runs out
  • ✓ Every access and update logged automatically
  • ✓ Lab results linked to patient, flagged and reportable

How we work

What we stand for

Patient safety first

Drug allergy warnings, controlled substance tracking, and full audit logs aren't optional extras — they're non-negotiable foundations. Every design decision starts with: does this make patient care safer?

Local-first, not locally-adapted

Insurance schemes, local payment methods, essential medicines lists — these are defaults, not add-ons. We didn't start from a Western system. We started from how healthcare is actually delivered in emerging markets.

Transparent pricing, no surprises

The price on the pricing page is the price you pay. No per-seat fees that balloon as you hire staff. No "enterprise module" that turns out to be the thing you actually need. Everything is included.

Built for non-IT teams

A hospital administrator should be able to add a new staff member, rename a department, or update the facility logo without calling us. If something requires our help to do, we consider it a product failure.

Security without shortcuts

Industry-standard password encryption, secure session management, and strict facility data isolation enforced at multiple layers. We don't cut corners on security because patient data is never ours to risk.

We're in it for the long term

Healthcare software you can't rely on is worse than no software at all. We're building KayCare to still be running your hospital in ten years — with CI/CD deployments, automated tests, and infrastructure that scales.
