Cookie Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how KayCare HMS ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on this marketing website (kaycarehms.com and related pages). It does not cover the KayCare HMS application itself, which has its own separate privacy and security documentation.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, remember your preferences, and provide information to the site owner. Some cookies are deleted when you close your browser (session cookies); others remain on your device for a set period (persistent cookies).

What cookies does this site use?

This marketing website uses a minimal set of cookies. We do not use any advertising, tracking, or analytics cookies. The table below lists the cookies in use:

Name Type Purpose Duration
kc-cookies Essential (localStorage) Remembers that you have dismissed the cookie notice so it does not reappear on every page. Until you clear browser storage
Formspree session Essential (third-party) Set by Formspree when you submit the demo request form. Used for spam prevention (CSRF protection). Only set when you interact with the form on the Book a Demo page. Session
Tailwind CDN Functional (third-party) This site loads the Tailwind CSS framework from a CDN. The CDN provider may set a session cookie for load-balancing purposes. No personal data is collected. Session

What we do not use

  • No Google Analytics or any other analytics platform
  • No advertising or retargeting pixels (Google Ads, Meta Pixel, etc.)
  • No social media tracking cookies
  • No session recording or heatmap tools

If we introduce any of the above in the future, this policy will be updated and a new consent notice displayed before those cookies are set.

Legal basis

The cookies listed above are either strictly necessary for the site to function or set by essential third-party services you interact with directly (such as submitting a form). Under GDPR and equivalent regulations, strictly necessary cookies do not require prior consent. Our cookie notice is provided as a transparency measure, not a consent gate.

How to control or delete cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Instructions for the most common browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data

Note: blocking or deleting cookies may affect how this site works (e.g. the cookie notice will reappear on each visit).

Third-party services

This site uses the following third-party services that may set cookies or process data when you use them:

  • Formspree (demo request form) — processes form submissions on our behalf. See Formspree's Privacy Policy.
  • Tailwind CSS CDN — serves CSS and JavaScript assets. No personal data is collected by this CDN beyond standard server logs.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
