What's included

Everything a pharmacy needs, nothing it doesn't

From walk-in retail sales to hospital prescription dispensing — PharmOS handles every workflow a modern pharmacy runs.

Point of Sale

Fast drug search, add-to-cart, and payment processing. Supports cash, card, and charge-to-patient-bill workflows. Receipt printing built in.

  • Walk-in retail sales
  • Prescription dispense integration
  • Auto stock deduction on sale

Drug Inventory

Track stock levels by name, dosage form, and strength. Set reorder thresholds and get instant alerts when stock runs low. Full movement history per drug.

  • Current stock per drug
  • Adjustments, returns, write-offs
  • Category grouping & filters

Purchase Orders

Create draft orders, send to suppliers, and receive goods with per-item quantity tracking. Stock automatically updates when goods arrive.

  • Draft → Ordered → Received flow
  • Partial receipt support
  • Auto stock movement on receive

Supplier Management

Maintain a supplier directory with contact details and purchase history. Link suppliers to purchase orders for full traceability from order to shelf.

  • Contact details & notes
  • Linked purchase history
  • Deactivate with data preserved

Controlled Substance Register

Every movement of a controlled substance is logged with pharmacist identity, timestamp, and reference. PDF register export for regulatory submissions.

  • Immutable CS audit trail
  • PDF register export
  • Per-drug running balance

Reorder Alerts

Set reorder thresholds per drug. The dashboard highlights every item below threshold with the deficit quantity — so you know exactly what to order and how much.

  • Per-drug threshold configuration
  • Deficit column shows shortfall
  • Grouped by drug category

Run PharmOS independently as a retail pharmacy system. Or pair it with KayCare HMS and unlock deep integration: prescriptions dispensed in HMS automatically deduct stock in PharmOS. No double entry, no manual sync.
